# Demo video script — 3 minutes + 30 seconds

**Builder:** Yonathan Zeitoune Mattout  
**Live product:** https://huella-evidencia.yonazet.chatgpt.site

Use this as a route map, not a text to read word for word. Keep the live URL visible and click as the merchant would.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem and user

“This is Huella. My user is an informal seasonal merchant who repeatedly buys inventory but cannot turn that activity into evidence a formal institution will act on. Huella does not create a credit score. It creates a narrow, source-linked operating record.”

## 0:20–0:45 — Commitment before data

Click **Ver el compromiso primero**.

“Before the user shares anything, the simulated institution must commit to a consequence: qualifying evidence moves the request past automatic rejection to human inventory-financing review. The screen also excludes contacts, family, conversations, customers, location and neighborhood data.”

Check the acknowledgement and continue.

## 0:45–1:15 — Invented evidence and simulated AI

Select all three receipts and click **Extraer campos con IA simulada**.

“Every receipt is invented and visibly labeled. The AI output is simulated. It extracts only supplier, date and total into structured data. Extraction is not verification.”

## 1:15–1:45 — User correction

Open review. Briefly change a total, change it back, then confirm the three sources.

“The user can inspect and correct every field. Nothing advances until all three images are checked. Inputs have limits, and the logic rejects impossible or future dates.”

Generate the record.

## 1:45–2:25 — Record, condition 3 and shadow clause

“The first message says this is not credit approval. The record shows three source-backed recurring purchases, not sales, profit or repayment capacity. My declaration most directly honors Condition 3: every gap is visible and has a repair path. Missing evidence is never converted into negative evidence.”

Open one source link and the JSON panel.

“The structured record preserves provenance and contains no score or association fields.”

## 2:25–3:00 — Consent, action and appeal

Continue to sharing.

“Consent is specific to one named recipient and repeats the promised action. Refusal creates no penalty. If the user shares, the only result is human review.”

Share, then open the correction form.

“The appeal goes to a person with authority to correct evidence and change the next step. The app never guesses why a black-box lender made a decision.”

## 3:00–3:30 — What changed my mind

“I began the week imagining a broad evidence passport that could prove repayment capacity. The argument and build changed my mind. Three receipts can truthfully support recurring operating activity, but not cash flow or creditworthiness. The strongest version is narrower: evidence only has value when its limits are visible and an institution commits in advance to a real next step.”
